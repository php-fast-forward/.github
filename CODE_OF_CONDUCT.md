# PHP Fast Forward community Code of Conduct

PHP Fast Forward welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Help make its repositories and project discussions useful places to learn and contribute. These are organization defaults; respect additional expectations published in the space where you participate.

## How we work together

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Make room for questions, acknowledge useful corrections, and respect others' time and boundaries. Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and the tradeoffs under discussion.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, repeated unwanted contact, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam or targeted humiliation.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by PHP Fast Forward maintainers. They also apply when someone is representing the project in a public setting.

## Reporting concerns

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on the relevant issue, pull request, or comment when available. GitHub may offer reporting to repository admins or to GitHub Support, depending on the context and repository settings. See [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for available reporting routes.

Use a repository's published confidential conduct contact if it provides one. These defaults do not establish a confidential contact. When no private contact is published, use GitHub's platform reporting tools for sensitive cases instead of posting personal details or a sensitive account in a public issue. Share only the information needed for the selected route; do not promise confidentiality on behalf of the project.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. They should consider context and severity, explain decisions where appropriate, and limit the circulation of sensitive information to people who need it to address the concern.

The aim is a respectful, constructive community. Maintainers retain responsibility for enforcing expectations in the spaces they manage; GitHub handles reports about its platform rules through its own processes.
